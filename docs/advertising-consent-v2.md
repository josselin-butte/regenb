## Choices and recipient

Recipient: Meta Platforms Ireland Limited. All optional choices default off. Reject all, Accept all and Customise have equal prominence. Closing the first banner records refusal. Analytics and external-content choices remain separate and have no configured services. Closing settings without saving preserves the current choice. Acceptance/refusal lasts six calendar months. This version invalidates earlier choices so they cannot authorise advertising.

Withdrawal immediately revokes the browser Pixel, removes accessible _fbp/_fbc cookies and reloads. Other tabs receive a BroadcastChannel notification and re-read the shared cookie; focus/periodic revalidation remains available. No blocked events are replayed. Cookies on Meta domains cannot be deleted by this site.
